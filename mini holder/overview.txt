An ongoing mechanical design project focused on developing an adjustable miniature holder for securely positioning small models during painting and display. The project involves designing and assembling the holder in SolidWorks, including multiple CAD components, engineering drawings, a rotational pivot, and a ball-detent locking mechanism for adjustable positioning. The design is being developed with a combination of 3D-printed parts and off-the-shelf mechanical components. Current work includes refining the CAD models, preparing components for fabrication, and evaluating the design for fit, functionality, and ease of use.
